Start here

Say hi to the bot. That opens Home — today's totals and every button you need. /start, /home and /menu all do the same thing.

hi
👋 Here's today
🍽️ 2 meals, 640 cal · ⚖️ 72.4 kg · 📖 45 min · ✅ Habits 3/5
🍽️ Log meal⚖️ Weight ✅ Habits💊 Supplements 🏋️ Workout📖 Study 🗒️ Recent📊 Analytics
Do this once, first

The bot only knows food you've saved. Save one thing you eat often, then logging it later takes one tap:
/food add oats per=100g kcal=380 p=13 c=67 f=7

Or don't — type a meal out the first time, and when it saves you'll be offered 💾 Save “…”. One tap keeps it, priced at exactly what you just logged, and the next time is a tap.

Log a meal

Fastest — tap it

Tap 🍽️ Log meal, pick the meal type, then pick the food. Rows marked ⚡ already know your usual amount and save the moment you tap them.

Type the whole thing

/describe 2 eggs, 100g oats

You get a preview first. Nothing is saved until you tap a meal type.

Same meal as last time

Tap 🔁 Repeat last meal. It copies your previous meal exactly.

/describe 2 eggs, 100g oats, quinoa
📝 Ready to log
• 2 eggs — 156 cal
• 100 g oats — 380 cal
Total: 536 cal

⚠️ Not logged
• quinoa — not in your foods
🌅 Breakfast🥗 Lunch 🍽️ Dinner🍎 Snack

Anything the bot doesn't recognise is listed as not logged and left out — it never guesses a calorie number. Save that food with /food add, then describe it again.

Check off habits

Fastest

Tap ✅ Habits, then tap the habit's name to tick it. Tap again to untick.

Set them up

/habits setup — type a name to add one, tap ❌ to remove. Send /cancel when you're done.

Forgot yesterday? Tap ← Yesterday on the checklist and tick it there. A streak counts back from today, so an unticked today shows a streak of 0.

Track supplements

Fastest

Tap 💊 Supplements and tap each one you've taken. Works exactly like habits.

Set them up

/supplements setup

Type Magnesium, or add detail: Vitamin D3, 2 capsules, with breakfast. Dose and timing are optional.

Twice a day?

In setup, tap 🎯 next to a supplement and pick 2×. Its row then counts — 1/2, 2/2 — and each tap adds one instead of toggling; ➖ takes one back off. The day counts as done only when you hit the target.

Supplements only record whether you took them. They never count toward calories or macros.

Log your weight

Fastest

Tap ⚖️ Weight. Five numbers appear around your last weigh-in — tap the one on the scale. If it isn't there, type it.

One message

/weight 72.4

One number per day — weighing again just corrects the day. Missing a day is fine: the chart carries your last reading forward for up to 10 days so the 7-day average keeps working, and draws those days hollow so you can see which ones you actually weighed.

Fix a mistake

  • /undo — removes your most recent entry. It shows you what it's about to delete and asks first.
  • /cancel — gets you out of anything half-finished. Use this whenever you feel stuck.
  • /recent — check whether something actually saved.
  • Tapped a ⚡ row by accident? It saved immediately — use /undo.
If the bot stops responding to buttons

Guided steps time out after 15 minutes and old buttons stop working. Say hi to get a fresh Home and start again.

See how you're doing

  • /summary — today at a glance.
  • /summary week — the last seven days.
  • /streak — your current habit streaks.
  • /chart habits — also diet, study, gym.

Where a food has no calorie information saved, totals say partial rather than quietly counting it as zero.

Two optional extras

These are off unless switched on, and the bot will tell you plainly if they aren't available.

  • Voice notes. Send a voice note from Home instead of typing. It's transcribed on the home computer, deleted straight after, and never sent anywhere. You still confirm the meal before it saves.
  • AI help reading meals — /aiparse on. Lets the bot understand phrases like "two eggs and a slice of toast". Only the words it couldn't read are sent; your logs, totals and name never are. Calories still come only from your saved foods — the AI never supplies a nutrition number. /aiparse off turns it back off.

Three things that are always true

Your entries are private to you. Calories are never invented — unknown stays unknown. Anything that saves instantly can be reversed with /undo.
